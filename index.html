Pricing

What a tattoo costs

Every artist at Splash of Ink sets their own prices. Your price depends on three things: the size of the piece, where it goes on your body, and how much detail it has. Tell your artist about your idea and they’ll send you a price range.

Placement

The same design costs more on the ribs, stomach, neck, hands and feet. Those spots take longer and need a steadier hand.

Big pieces

Larger work can be split into sessions to fit your budget. Your artist will plan it with you.

Credit for coming back

Spend over $150 and you build a $100 tattoo credit, to use that day or on your next visit.
